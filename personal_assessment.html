Captcha & Threat-Mitigation Sprints

After validating the plan, I spent ten concentrated hours across October 24, 25, November 1, 2, 6, and 7 implementing the CAPTCHA layer in the redesigned UI, writing a reproducible tutorial, and filming the security narrative for our proof-of-concept videos. Every artifact referenced the same threat model so documentation, code, and video stayed consistent.

Reflection

The through-line across all 33.5 hours is disciplined security ownership paired with tangible learning aids. I proved that AI-generated code can be production-ready when paired with rigorous human auditing, clear documentation, and responsive collaboration. By delivering hardened authentication, reusable tutorials, and consistent meeting leadership, I made our project safer, easier to adopt, and better documented—exactly the impact a security and documentation lead should have when the stakes are high and deadlines are tight.
